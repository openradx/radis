{% if form.fields.mode %}

Choose a mode in the search bar. Full-text finds exactly the reports that contain your keywords, with the matched words highlighted.

Semantic locks the keyword box and searches by the description alone: the result is the list of reports whose meaning is closest to it, in similarity order, without highlighting. Operators, quotes and NOT have no special meaning in a description. The optional "Minimum similarity" filter cuts the list where the similarity drops below the value you enter.

Newly imported reports are embedded in the background and do not appear in semantic mode until then.

{% else %}

Provide your search query in the search box above to search for reports. You can use additional filters to narrow down the search results (on the right side).

{% endif %}

The following rules apply to keywords:

  • Search terms are case-insensitive.
  • All terms in a query must be matched by a document (implicit AND query).
  • term1 OR term2 (capital OR) means that either term1 or term2 must match.
  • A phrase surrounded by quotes will look for an exact match, e.g. "lung disease".
  • NOT in front of a term means that the term or phrase must not be present. A minus sign does not exclude.
